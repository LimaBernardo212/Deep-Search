REFUND POLICY

Last Updated: January 14, 2026

1. INTRODUCTION

At Log, we want you to be completely satisfied with every transaction. This Refund Policy explains our commitment to fair and transparent refund practices. We've designed our policy to protect both customers and service providers while ensuring a positive experience for everyone on our platform.

2. CANCELLATION & REFUND POLICY

2.1. Standard Refund Rate

When you cancel an appointment, you receive an 80% refund of the total amount paid.

Why 80%?

  • The service provider has already reserved time for your appointment
  • They may have turned away other customers
  • Administrative costs and payment processing fees have been incurred
  • The 20% retention compensates the business for the lost booking opportunity

2.2. Refund Breakdown Example

If you paid R$ 100,00 for a service and cancel:

  • Refund to You: R$ 80,00 (80%)
  • Service Provider Keeps: R$ 20,00 (20%)
  • Processing Time: 5-10 business days to appear in your account

2.3. Who Processes the Refund

Refunds are processed automatically through our system:

  • Automatic Processing: Refund initiated immediately upon cancellation
  • Stripe Handling: Refund processed through Stripe's secure system
  • Original Payment Method: Money returns to the card/account you used
  • No Manual Requests: Simply cancel your appointment to receive your refund

3. HOW TO CANCEL & RECEIVE A REFUND

3.1. Cancellation Process

To cancel an appointment and receive your refund:

  1. Log into Your Account: Access your Log dashboard
  2. Go to 'My Appointments': Find your scheduled services
  3. Select the Appointment: Click on the booking you want to cancel
  4. Click 'Cancel Appointment': Confirm your cancellation
  5. Automatic Refund: Your 80% refund is processed immediately
  6. Confirmation Email: You'll receive a refund confirmation email

3.2. Cancellation Deadlines

You can cancel anytime before your scheduled appointment. However:

  • Recommended: Cancel at least 24 hours in advance when possible
  • Last-Minute Cancellations: Still receive 80% refund, but discouraged
  • No-Show Policy: If you don't show up, no refund is available
  • Be Courteous: Early cancellations help businesses fill the time slot

3.3. What Happens After Cancellation

Once you cancel:

  • Immediate Confirmation: You receive a cancellation confirmation email
  • Provider Notification: The service provider is notified automatically
  • Refund Initiation: Stripe processes the 80% refund within minutes
  • Balance Adjustment: Provider's account is updated immediately
  • Time Slot Released: The appointment slot becomes available for others

4. REFUND TIMELINE

4.1. Processing Time

Refunds follow this timeline:

  • Immediate: Refund initiated in our system when you cancel
  • 1-3 Business Days: Stripe processes the refund transaction
  • 5-10 Business Days: Funds appear in your bank account/card
  • Bank Dependent: Your bank may take additional time to process

4.2. Why It Takes Time

The refund process involves multiple parties:

  • Log Platform: Initiates the refund immediately
  • Stripe: Processes the refund and communicates with banks (1-3 days)
  • Your Bank: Receives funds and credits your account (2-7 days)
  • Card Networks: Visa/Mastercard processing times vary

4.3. Checking Refund Status

You can track your refund:

  • Log Dashboard: View refund status in your transaction history
  • Email Updates: Confirmation emails at each stage
  • Bank Statement: Refund appears as "LOG* REFUND [service name]"
  • Support: Contact us if refund doesn't appear within 10 business days

5. SUBSCRIPTION REFUNDS

5.1. Subscription Cancellation

For monthly subscriptions:

  • Cancel Anytime: No penalties or fees for cancellation
  • Current Period: Access continues until end of current billing cycle
  • No Pro-Rated Refunds: Subscriptions are non-refundable once the billing cycle starts
  • Future Charges: No further charges after the current

    5.2. How to Cancel Subscriptions

    1. Go to 'My Plans' in your account dashboard
    2. Select the subscription you want to cancel
    3. Click 'Cancel Subscription'
    4. Confirm cancellation
    5. Receive confirmation email

    5.3. Subscription Billing Disputes

    If you believe you were charged incorrectly:

    • Contact us immediately at log.tools.app@gmail.com
    • Provide your transaction ID and explain the issue
    • We'll investigate within 48 hours
    • Legitimate billing errors will be refunded in full

6. SPECIAL CIRCUMSTANCES

6.1. Service Provider Cancellation

If the service provider cancels your appointment:

  • 100% Full Refund: You receive your complete payment back
  • Immediate Processing: Refund processed automatically
  • No Penalties: No fees or charges to you
  • Apology: We're sorry for the inconvenience

6.2. Service Not Provided

If you paid but the service wasn't delivered:

  • Contact support immediately with details
  • We'll investigate and verify your claim
  • Full refund issued if service wasn't provided
  • Provider may face penalties for non-delivery

6.3. Unsatisfactory Service

If you're unhappy with the service quality:

  • First Contact: Try to resolve with the service provider directly
  • Mediation: Log can help mediate disputes
  • Case-by-Case: Refunds considered based on circumstances
  • Documentation: Photos or evidence may be required

6.4. Technical Issues

If technical problems prevented service delivery:

  • Report the issue immediately
  • Provide details of the technical problem
  • We'll investigate and determine cause
  • Refunds issued if fault lies with our platform

7. NO-SHOW POLICY

7.1. Customer No-Show

If you don't show up for your appointment:

  • No Refund: 0% refund for confirmed no-shows
  • Full Payment Retained: Service provider keeps 100% of payment
  • Account Warning: Repeated no-shows may result in restrictions
  • Prevention: Always cancel if you can't attend

7.2. Late Arrivals

If you arrive significantly late:

  • Service provider may refuse service (no refund)
  • Service may be shortened (no refund for lost time)
  • Call ahead if you're running late
  • Provider's decision is final regarding late arrivals

8. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

8.1. Filing a Dispute

If you have a payment or refund dispute:

  1. Contact Support: Email log.tools.app@gmail.com
  2. Provide Details: Transaction ID, appointment details, issue description
  3. Evidence: Include any relevant photos, messages, or documentation
  4. Timeframe: File disputes within 14 days of the transaction

8.2. Investigation Process

When you file a dispute:

  • 48-Hour Review: We investigate within 2 business days
  • Both Parties: We contact both you and the service provider
  • Evidence Review: All documentation is carefully reviewed
  • Fair Resolution: Decisions made based on facts and policies
  • Final Decision: Resolution communicated via email

8.3. Chargeback Policy

If you initiate a chargeback with your bank:

  • Preferred Resolution: Please contact us first before filing chargebacks
  • Our Response: We'll provide transaction evidence to your bank
  • Account Suspension: Your account may be suspended during investigation
  • Unjustified Chargebacks: May result in account termination
  • Fees: You may be liable for chargeback fees if dispute is unjustified

9. REFUND LIMITATIONS

9.1. Non-Refundable Items

The following are non-refundable:

  • Completed services (unless service was unsatisfactory)
  • Subscription fees (except billing errors)
  • No-show appointments
  • Services cancelled by customer after delivery
  • Third-party fees (Stripe processing fees)

9.2. Refund Restrictions

Refunds may be denied if:

  • Appointment was marked as completed by provider
  • You didn't show up for the appointment
  • Cancellation violates provider's specific terms
  • Evidence of service delivery exists
  • Request is made more than 14 days after the transaction

10. FOR SERVICE PROVIDERS

10.1. Impact of Customer Cancellations

When a customer cancels:

  • 80% Deduction: Your balance is reduced by the refund amount
  • 20% Retention: You keep 20% as cancellation compensation
  • Immediate Update: Your account balance updates instantly
  • Notification: You receive an email about the cancellation
  • Slot Availability: The time slot is freed for new bookings

10.2. Your Cancellation Rights

As a service provider, you can:

  • Cancel appointments in emergency situations
  • Customer receives 100% refund when you cancel
  • You don't receive payment for cancelled appointments
  • Excessive cancellations may affect your account standing

10.3. Setting Your Own Policies

While you must follow our 80% refund policy, you can:

  • Communicate your preferred cancellation notice period
  • Set expectations with customers about cancellations
  • Offer voluntary full refunds at your discretion
  • Note: You cannot restrict refunds below our 80% minimum

11. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q1: Why don't I get a full 100% refund when I cancel?

A: The 20% retention compensates service providers for reserving time, turning away other customers, and administrative costs. This is standard in the service industry.

Q2: Can I get a full refund in an emergency?

A: Our standard policy is 80%. However, contact support with emergency documentation, and we'll review on a case-by-case basis.

Q3: What if the service provider cancels on me?

A: You receive a full 100% refund immediately, no questions asked.

Q4: How long until I see the money back in my account?

A: Typically 5-10 business days, though it can be faster depending on your bank.

Q5: Can I cancel a subscription mid-month for a refund?

A: No, subscriptions are non-refundable once the billing cycle starts. You'll have access until the end of the current period.

Q6: What happens if I don't show up?

A: No refund is available for no-shows. The service provider keeps 100% of the payment.

Q7: Can I dispute a refund decision?

A: Yes, contact support with additional information or evidence, and we'll review your case.

Q8: Does Log keep any of the refund?

A: No. When you cancel, you get 80% back, the provider keeps 20%. Log doesn't take any additional fees from refunds.
