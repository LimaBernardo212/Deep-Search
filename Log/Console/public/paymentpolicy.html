PAYMENT TRANSPARENCY POLICY

Last Updated: January 14, 2026

1. INTRODUCTION

At Log, we believe in complete transparency regarding how we handle your money. This Payment Transparency Policy explains our payment processing methods, fee structures, security measures, and how funds are managed when you use our service. We are committed to maintaining the highest standards of financial integrity and ensuring you understand exactly where your money goes.

2. PAYMENT PROCESSING PARTNER

2.1. Stripe Integration

All payments on Log are processed through Stripe, Inc., a globally trusted and PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment processor. We chose Stripe because:

  • Industry-leading security standards and compliance certifications
  • Advanced fraud detection and prevention systems
  • Support for multiple payment methods and currencies
  • Proven track record with millions of businesses worldwide
  • Transparent fee structure and reliable service

2.2. Why We Don't Process Payments Directly

Log does not directly process or store your payment information. By using Stripe:

  • Your financial data is protected by bank-level security
  • We never see or store your complete card details
  • You benefit from Stripe's continuous security monitoring
  • Your payment information is encrypted end-to-end
  • We minimize our liability and your risk exposure

2.3. Stripe's Credentials

Stripe maintains the following certifications and compliance standards:

  • PCI Service Provider Level 1 (highest level of certification)
  • SOC 1 and SOC 2 Type II certified
  • ISO 27001 certified for information security
  • Compliant with GDPR, CCPA, and other privacy regulations

3. FEE STRUCTURE & TRANSPARENCY

3.1. Our Pricing Philosophy

We believe in simple, transparent pricing with no hidden fees. What you see is what you pay.

3.2. Platform Fees for Store Owners

Log charges a 3% (0.003) platform fee on each transaction. This minimal fee helps us:

  • Maintain and improve platform infrastructure
  • Provide 24/7 customer support
  • Ensure secure payment processing
  • Develop new features for your business
  • Cover operational and hosting costs

3.3. Complete Fee Breakdown

When a customer pays R$ 100,00 for a service:

  • Total Payment: R$ 100,00
  • Stripe Processing Fee: ~R$ 4,00 (3.4% + R$ 0.60)
  • Log Platform Fee: R$ 3,00(3%)
  • Store Owner Receives: R$ 95,70 (95.7%)

Note: Stripe's fees vary by payment method:

  • Credit/Debit Card: 3.4% + R$ 0.60
  • PIX: 0.99% (lower fee)
  • Boleto: R$ 3.49 flat fee

3.4. Subscription Fees

When customers subscribe to your plans:

  • Monthly Subscriptions: 5% platform fee applies
  • First Payment: Processed immediately
  • Recurring Charges: Automatic monthly billing
  • No Setup Fees: Free to create subscription plans
  • No Cancellation Fees: Customers can cancel anytime

3.5. What We Don't Charge

To maintain transparency, here's what we explicitly DO NOT charge:

  • Account creation or maintenance fees
  • Monthly minimums or quotas
  • Listing fees for services
  • Fees to withdraw your earnings
  • Fees to download or export your data
  • Fees to close your account
  • Hidden processing fees
  • Surprise price increases mid-billing cycle

3.6. Currency and Conversion

All transactions are processed in Brazilian Reais (BRL). If your bank account or card is in a different currency:

  • Your bank or card issuer will handle the conversion
  • Exchange rates are determined by your financial institution
  • We do not charge additional currency conversion fees
  • Your card issuer may charge their own foreign transaction fees

4. PAYMENT SECURITY

4.1. Data Encryption

All payment data transmitted through our service is encrypted using:

  • TLS 1.2+: Industry-standard encryption for data in transit
  • AES-256: Military-grade encryption for data at rest
  • Tokenization: Your card details are replaced with secure tokens
  • End-to-End Encryption: Data is encrypted from your device to Stripe's servers

4.2. What We Store

For your security, we have strict limitations on what payment information we store:

  • We DO store: Transaction IDs, payment status, subscription details, receipt information
  • We DO NOT store: Full card numbers, CVV codes, card PINs, complete bank account details
  • Tokenized Data: We only store secure tokens provided by Stripe, not actual card data

4.3. Fraud Prevention

We implement multiple layers of fraud protection:

  • Stripe Radar for real-time fraud detection using machine learning
  • 3D Secure authentication for added verification when needed
  • Address Verification System (AVS) checks
  • Velocity checks to detect unusual payment patterns
  • Manual review of suspicious transactions
  • Immediate alerts for potential fraudulent activity

4.4. Secure Payment Methods

We support the following secure payment methods:

  • Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express)
  • Debit cards
  • Boleto Bancário (Brazil)
  • Digital wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay) - where available

5. HOW PAYMENTS WORK

5.1. One-Time Payments

When you make a one-time payment for a service:

  • Step 1: You're redirected to Stripe's secure checkout
  • Step 2: You enter your payment details (protected by Stripe)
  • Step 3: Stripe processes the payment and verifies your card
  • Step 4: Upon approval, you're redirected back to Log
  • Step 5: You receive immediate confirmation and a receipt
  • Step 6: The service provider is notified of your booking

5.2. Subscription Payments

For recurring subscriptions:

  • Initial Charge: First payment processed immediately upon subscription
  • Recurring Charges: Automatic billing on the same day each month
  • Billing Reminders: Email notification 3 days before each charge
  • Failed Payments: Automatic retry with notification if payment fails
  • Grace Period: 7-day grace period to update payment method

5.3. Payouts to Store Owners

If you're a store owner receiving payments:

  • Payout Schedule: On-demand withdrawal, anytime you want
  • Minimum Threshold: R$ 20,00 minimum for payout processing
  • Processing Time: 2-5 business days to reach your bank account
  • Transparent Reporting: Detailed breakdown of all transactions and fees
  • Instant Access: View pending and available balance in real-time
  • Automatic Deductions: Platform and Stripe fees are deducted before payout

6. BILLING TRANSPARENCY

6.1. Clear Billing Statements

Every transaction appears on your bank statement as:

  • Merchant Name: "LOG*" followed by the service or plan name
  • Amount: Exact charge in BRL
  • Date: Transaction date
  • Reference Number: Unique transaction ID for your records

6.2. Receipt and Invoice Access

You always have access to:

  • Digital receipts sent immediately after each transaction
  • Downloadable invoices in PDF format
  • Complete transaction history in your account dashboard
  • Itemized breakdowns of all charges and fees
  • Annual statements for tax purposes

6.3. Billing Notifications

We keep you informed with:

  • Email confirmation immediately after each successful payment
  • Reminder emails 3 days before subscription renewals
  • Notifications of failed payment attempts
  • Alerts when subscription plans change or expire
  • Receipts with detailed transaction information
