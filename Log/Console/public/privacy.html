PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: January 14, 2026

1. INTRODUCTION

Welcome to Log's Privacy Policy. This policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use our marketplace platform. Log connects customers with service providers for booking, scheduling, and payment processing. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal data.

By using Log, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. This policy applies to all users, including both customers booking services and service providers offering their services.

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

2.1. Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Basic Information: Name, email address, phone number
  • Authentication: Password (encrypted) or third-party OAuth tokens (Google)
  • Profile Data: Optional profile picture and preferences

2.2. Service Provider Information

If you're a service provider, we also collect:

  • Business Information: Store name, description, address, operating hours
  • Service Listings: Service descriptions, photos, pricing, duration
  • Team Information: Staff names, emails, photos (for display to customers)
  • Banking Information: Bank account details for payouts (securely stored by Stripe)

2.3. Transaction Information

When you book or provide services:

  • Booking Details: Service type, date, time, location, special requests
  • Payment Information: Processed and stored by Stripe (we never store full card numbers)
  • Transaction History: Appointment records, payments, cancellations, refunds
  • Communications: Messages between customers and service providers

2.4. Automatically Collected Information

When you use our platform, we automatically collect:

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, browser type and version
  • Usage Data: Pages viewed, features used, time spent, interaction patterns
  • Log Data: IP address, access times, referring URLs
  • Cookies: See Section 8 for details
  • Location Data: General location based on IP address (not precise GPS)

2.5. Information from Third Parties

We receive information from:

  • Google OAuth: Name, email, profile picture (when you sign in with Google)
  • Stripe: Payment confirmation, refund status, payout information

3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

3.1. Platform Services

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Connect customers with service providers
  • Process bookings and appointments
  • Handle payments and refunds through Stripe
  • Send appointment confirmations and reminders
  • Manage subscription plans
  • Process payouts to service providers

3.2. Communication

We use your contact information to:

  • Send booking confirmations and updates
  • Notify you of appointment changes or cancellations
  • Send password reset codes and security alerts
  • Provide customer support
  • Send service-related announcements
  • Notify service providers of new bookings
  • Send promotional communications (with your consent)

3.3. Platform Improvement

We analyze usage data to:

  • Understand how users interact with our platform
  • Improve search and discovery features
  • Develop new features and services
  • Fix bugs and technical issues
  • Optimize performance and user experience
  • Create anonymous analytics and reports

3.4. Security and Fraud Prevention

We use your information to:

  • Verify your identity
  • Detect and prevent fraud, payment disputes, and chargebacks
  • Monitor for suspicious activity
  • Enforce our Terms and Conditions
  • Protect user safety and platform integrity
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. HOW WE SHARE YOUR INFORMATION

4.1. We Do Not Sell Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes. Your data is yours.

4.2. Between Platform Users

To facilitate bookings:

  • Customers see: Service provider name, business info, services, pricing, photos
  • Service Providers see: Customer name, contact info for booked appointments
  • Both parties see: Appointment details, booking history (for their mutual bookings)

4.3. Payment Processing

Stripe, Inc. processes all payments on our platform:

  • We share transaction details with Stripe to process payments
  • Stripe stores your payment information securely
  • We never see or store your complete card numbers or CVV codes
  • Stripe's use of your information is governed by their Privacy Policy
  • Bank account information for service providers is encrypted and stored by Stripe

4.4. Service Providers

We share information with trusted service providers who help us operate:

  • MongoDB Atlas: Database hosting and data storage
  • Stripe: Payment processing and payout management
  • Email Services: Transactional emails and notifications
  • Cloud Hosting: Application hosting and delivery

These providers are contractually obligated to protect your information.

4.5. Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information when required by law or to:

  • Comply with legal processes (subpoenas, court orders)
  • Respond to government or regulatory requests
  • Enforce our Terms and Conditions
  • Protect against fraud or security threats
  • Protect user safety and platform integrity

4.6. Business Transfers

If Log is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale, your information may be transferred. We will notify you before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

5. DATA SECURITY

5.1. Security Measures

We implement multiple layers of security:

  • Encryption: AES-256 for data at rest, TLS 1.2+ for data in transit
  • Authentication: Secure JWT tokens, bcrypt password hashing
  • Payment Security: PCI-DSS compliant through Stripe
  • Access Controls: Role-based permissions, authenticated endpoints
  • Monitoring: Real-time security alerts and logging
  • Regular Audits: Security reviews and vulnerability testing

5.2. Payment Information Security

Your payment data is exceptionally well-protected:

  • Stripe maintains PCI Service Provider Level 1 certification (highest level)
  • Card data is tokenized - we only store secure tokens, never actual card numbers
  • Bank account details are encrypted at the highest level
  • All payment communications use end-to-end encryption

5.3. Your Responsibility

You must:

  • Use a strong, unique password
  • Never share your login credentials
  • Log out on shared devices
  • Notify us immediately of unauthorized access
  • Keep your contact information up to date

5.4. Limitations

While we implement robust security measures, no internet transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we continually work to protect your data.

6. DATA RETENTION

6.1. Active Accounts

We retain your information while your account is active and as needed to provide services.

6.2. Transaction Records

We retain transaction records for:

  • Active Users: Complete history available indefinitely
  • Closed Accounts: 7 years for legal, tax, and compliance purposes
  • Dispute Resolution: Extended retention during active disputes

6.3. Legitimate Business Purposes

After account closure, we may retain certain information for:

  • Compliance with legal and tax obligations
  • Fraud prevention and security
  • Resolving disputes or enforcing agreements
  • Anonymous analytics and aggregated statistics

6.4. Deletion Requests

You may request deletion of your data. We'll delete within 30 days, except where retention is legally required.

7. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

7.1. Access Your Data

You can:

  • View your account information in settings
  • Access your transaction history
  • Request a complete copy of your data
  • Export your booking and payment records

7.2. Correct Your Data

You can update your information anytime through:

  • Account settings
  • Service provider dashboard
  • Contacting our support team

7.3. Delete Your Data

You have the right to:

  • Close your account
  • Request deletion of personal information
  • Remove payment methods
  • Delete service listings (providers)

Note: Some information may be retained for legal compliance.

7.4. Opt-Out Rights

You can opt out of:

  • Marketing emails (while still receiving booking confirmations)
  • Non-essential cookies
  • Data sharing for analytics (where permitted by law)

7.5. Data Portability

You can request your data in a portable format (CSV, JSON) for transfer to another service.

8. COOKIES AND TRACKING

8.1. What We Use

We use cookies and similar technologies for:

  • Authentication: Keep you logged in (essential)
  • Preferences: Remember your settings
  • Security: Protect against fraud and unauthorized access
  • Analytics: Understand platform usage (anonymized)

8.2. Types of Cookies

  • Essential Cookies: Required for login and security
  • Functional Cookies: Remember your preferences
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us improve the platform

8.3. Cookie Management

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling essential cookies will prevent you from using the platform.

9. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

9.1. Stripe Payment Processing

All payments are processed by Stripe, Inc.:

  • Stripe's Privacy Policy governs their use of your payment data
  • Stripe maintains the highest security certifications
  • We share only necessary transaction details with Stripe
  • Your payment information is protected by Stripe's security measures

9.2. Google OAuth

If you sign in with Google:

  • Google's Privacy Policy applies to their authentication service
  • We receive only basic profile information (name, email, photo)
  • You control what data Google shares through your Google account settings

9.3. Third-Party Links

Service provider listings may include external links. We're not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.

  • Request data portability

  • Information about the possibility of not providing consent

To exercise these rights, contact us at log.tools.app@gmail.com
